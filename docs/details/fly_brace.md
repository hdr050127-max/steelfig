# Fly brace: rafter bottom flange to purlin

**Parts**: angle each side of the rafter; a cleat plate in the corner of the rafter web and the top of the bottom
flange; one M12 8.8/S at each end.
**Rules** (`assembly.fly_braces_on`), from TTW-00-DR-ST-30202 Typical Fly Brace Detail:
- angle by rafter: 40x40x3 EA up to 310 UB, 50x50x3 to 410, 65x65x5 to 610, 75x75x5.5 above (drawn 75x75x6)
- always at 45 degrees; both bolts on the leg's centre line, which sets where it lands on the purlin
- 8 mm cleat plate on the web and the top of the bottom flange, reaching just past the flange tip: **nothing hangs
  below the flange** (rejected by the user 2026-11-04 when it did)
- purlin bolt in the lower web hole: B = 55 mm above the purlin bottom for 150-250 series (Stramit guide,
  `sources/stramit-guide.md`; TTW draws the same height); welding to purlins is not recommended
- long leg flat on the flange-free face of the purlin web: upslope face of a Z (its bottom flange is downslope), back of
  a C; the cleat face is in line with it, so the leg lies flat on both
**Sources**: TTW drawing above (course drawings, details indicative); purlin manufacturers' manuals for the purlin
orientation; AS 4100:2020 Cl 9.5 for the bolt distances.
**To verify**: the cleat size and the bolt heights are scaled from the drawing, which gives no dimension for them.
