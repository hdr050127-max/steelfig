# Pinned cap plate: rafter continuous over an SHS / UC column

**Parts**: cap plate on the roof slope; column cut parallel to the roof under it; four bolts just outside the
column walls (SHS) or flanges (UC) through the rafter bottom flange; rafter web stiffeners over the column walls.
**Rules**: cap thickness 16–25 mm by rafter depth; plate = column size + 160 mm along the rafter, flange width + 40
across; bolts 45 mm outside the walls (bolts cannot be inside a tube); bolt axis along the roof normal; stiffeners
over the walls because the wall reactions enter the rafter web as concentrated loads [AS 4100 Cl 5.14].
**Sources**: SCI P358 §2 (simple joint principle: bearing, nominally pinned); AS 4100 Cl 5.14, 9.5. See `SOURCES.md`.
**To verify**: plate overhang (160) and bolt offset (45) are ASSUMPTION; whether stiffeners are needed depends on
the reaction (always modelled).
