# Pinned cap plate: rafter continuous over an SHS / UC column

**Parts**: cap plate on the roof slope; column cut parallel to the roof under it; bolts just outside the column
walls (45 mm) through the rafter bottom flange; rafter web stiffeners over the column walls.
**Rules**: cap thickness 16–25 mm by rafter depth; plate = column size + 160 mm along the rafter, flange width + 40
across; bolt axis along the roof normal.
**Sources**: TTW ST-30xxx (haunch over SHS column); ASI standardised connections (column cap).
**To verify**: plate overhang (160 mm) and bolt offset (45 mm) are ASSUMPTION; stiffener need depends on the
reaction (modelled always).
