# Bolted end-plate splice (haunch to rafter, rafter to rafter, apex)

**Parts**: two end plates back to back, bolted above and below each flange of the shallower section; at a ridge
(apex) the same arrangement with vertical plates.
**Rules**: plate thickness and bolt by the shallower depth (`connections.defaults`); gauge 90 / 100 [SCI P398
§1.2]; rows ≥ 1.75 d_f from the plate end [AS 4100 Table 9.5.2]; plates span the deeper section + 50 mm each side;
each member trimmed to its plate face.
**Sources**: SCI P398 §4.4 bolted end plate splices; AS 4100 Cl 9.5. See `SOURCES.md`.
