# Bolted end-plate splice (haunch to rafter, rafter to rafter)

**Parts**: two end plates back to back; bolts above and below each flange of the shallower section; plates span
the deeper section plus 50 mm each side.
**Rules**: plate thickness and bolt by the shallower depth (`connections.defaults`); rows 45–65 mm from the flange;
each member trimmed to its plate face (x ∓ plate thickness).
**Sources**: TTW-00-DR-ST-30201 splice type 3 (90 gauge); ASI standardised connections (moment splice); AS 4100:2020 Cl 9.5.
**To verify**: whether the course detail uses four or six bolt rows on deep haunches.
